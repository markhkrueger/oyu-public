This system is used for controlling the hot water system for a home that has a hot water recirculation pump and a tankless hot water heater. It includes a flow sensor for detecthing if hot water is being used. The flow sensor is on connected after a one-way valve between the hot water circulation loop and the cold water source, so it measures flow of cold water into the hot water loop. The controller described here collects status of all the sensors and the pump and allows viewing them from a built-in HTTP server. When a hot water tap is turned on in the house, the flow of water will be detected and the recirculator pump will be turned on to allow efficient heating of the water in the loop, but avoid running the pump at times when water isn't being used. After the hot water is turned off and the flow stops, the pump will be turned off. For ease of integration with Apple HomeKit the sensor outputs and pump control are added via a HomeKit bridge to allow controlling the pump manually through the Apple Home app.
